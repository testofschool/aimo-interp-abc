Hey @everyone 

New: White-Box Awards ($1,000 per track)

While the competition also allows black-box and hybrid methods, we would also like to see more submissions experimenting with white-box signals as we think these signals can provide a generalizable source of information, but also teach us more about how reasoning in LLMs functions and how we should study it.

For this purpose, we’re reserving $2,000 of the prize pool for two special awards for the best white-box methods in the AIMO Interpretability Challenge: $1,000 in the Main track and $1,000 in the Small Models track.

What counts as white-box?
Your method’s verdict must depend on signals beyond the generated text. For example:

hidden-state activations, residual stream or attention patterns
weights, gradients or parameter-space analysis
probes, sparse autoencoders, transcoders or other learned readouts of internals
interventions such as activation patching, ablation or steering

Methods that rely only on generated text or chains of thought do not qualify.

How is it awarded?

In each track, the award goes to the highest-ranked white-box submission on the private test set.
Your technical report must show that the white-box signal matters: include an ablation comparing your method with and without its white-box component.
The organizers verify eligibility during the report review (Nov 15–30).
