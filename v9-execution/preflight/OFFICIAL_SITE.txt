AIMO (AI Mathematical Olympiad) is an annual competition of AI systems in solving unseen frontier mathematical problems.
            Thanks to its importance and large prizes ($2.2M USD this year), AIMO attracts huge community attention — last year, the competition was covered by global media The Wall Street Journal and Bloomberg ,
            and participated in by over 4,000+ teams . Our ambition is to turn this tremendous engineering effort into scientific knowledge — laying robust stepping stones that will accelerate progress in mathematical reasoning and in AI as a whole. We believe that interpretability research can play a key role in helping us achieve this goal — allowing us to understand the mechanisms of reasoning in SOTA AI systems and providing actionable knowledge for their future, open advancements. More broadly, the competition connects interpretability and generalization research around one of the most important questions in AI today: to what extent is the decision-making of frontier AI models generalizable and thus, reliable? The Challenge Robust or Spurious? The AIMO Interpretability Challenge requires participants to submit a system that decides, for a single problem–model pair , whether that model answers that problem robustly .
            The full problem formulation, data collection, and evaluation protocol are described in our competition proposal paper . Competition Rules Submissions must follow these rules, designed to prevent overfitting and keep the competition fair: Data [Clarification 19 Sep] We retired the rule that teams may not use extra labeled data. We disincentivise large-scale test-time perturbation generation through the inference time limit, but such approaches are not explicitly disallowed. Note that any attempts for leakage of evaluation data are still forbidden and will result in the disqualification. Signals: teams can use weights, activations, token probabilities, or any other model internal signals as inputs for their methods. While we encourage the development and submission of white-box methods, black-box behavioural analyses within the evaluation compute budget (eg based on models’ reasoning chains) are also permitted. Tracks: teams may submit to the Main or Small models track, but each submission will count only for the chosen submission track. Teams may choose to submit the same submission to both (Main and Small Models) tracks. Report: teams considered for awards must submit a technical report with details on their submission and its open-sourced implementation by November 5, 2026 (AoE) for compliance checks. The report must not exceed 5 pages, excluding the AI usage statement, limitations, references, and appendix. Transparency: teams considered for awards must enable the organizers to reproduce their score from the instructions in the technical report alone. The report must document the training data, the training codebase and the submission bundle, and the submission must depend solely on the documented resources. Reviewing: teams considered for awards commit to peer-review up to 3 technical reports of other teams during the reviewing period (November 5–12, 2026, AoE). Which materials should I follow? Use this website as the primary reference, the getting-started repository for the submission interface and the evaluation environment, and the competition proposal paper for background. Where the paper differs on specifics, the website and the repository are current. 2. Who can participate? The challenge is open to everyone — academic researchers, independent researchers, and industry practitioners alike. There are no restrictions on team size, affiliation, or career level. Students on all education levels are welcome! 3. Do I need to be a participant of AIMO to join? No. The AIMO Interpretability Challenge is a separate competition. You will be given access to AIMO model submissions as part of the provided environment — you do not need to submit to AIMO yourself. 4. What is the difference between the Main Track and the Small Models Track? The Main Track covers the full scale of top-performing models from AIMO 3 with no restriction on model size. The Small Models Track subsets the evaluation to the best-performing models below the 10-billion-parameter scale, offering a comparable setup for methods that are more compute-heavy to train — such as Sparse Autoencoders or Transcoders — where analysing very large models may be infeasible. 5. What runtime environment does my submission need? Submissions must be packaged as Codabench submission bundle and must conform to the unified interface defined in the getting-started repository . Containers are evaluated on our servers without internet access . Detailed technical documentation and a starter kit are available in the getting-started repository . 6. Which models will my submission be asked to analyse? The Main Track evaluates Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B , Skywork/Skywork-OR1-Math-7B , allenai/Olmo-3-7B-Think , deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B and openai/gpt-oss-120b ; the Small Models Track evaluates the same set without gpt-oss-120b. All are open-weights checkpoints cached on the evaluation workers, so any method can load the model it is asked to judge. 7. How is the robustness label defined? A case is robust when the model's accuracy holds up across every perturbation we apply, and non-robust when at least one degrades it clearly; the exact criterion differs from the outline in the proposal paper and is not published, because we want to disincentivise efforts to reconstruct the labels over label-agnostic methods. Every case in the validation sets is supported by a substantially large boundary betwen positives and negatives. 8. How much time does my submission get? Each submission has 3600 seconds for the entire prediction run, covering all models and all problems together, with the scoring run limited separately; the getting-started repository documents the environment in full. 9. Will there be tutorial material and a starter kit? Yes. A starter kit with worked examples and full documentation is available in the getting-started repository , with baseline implementations in the baselines repository . The problem formulation and evaluation methodology are described in our competition proposal paper , available now on arXiv. 10. Can I participate in both tracks? Yes, teams may submit to both the Main Track and the Small Models Track independently. 11. How is the winner determined? Submissions are ranked by accuracy in classifying each problem–model instance as robust or spurious on the held-out test set. The Main Track and Small Models Track are ranked separately. 12. What sampling/decoding settings were used to generate model predictions? We used temperature = 1.0 , top_k = 40 , and top_p = 0.95 to keep the answers diverse. 13. What is the maximum generation length, and how are responses that run out of budget scored? The maximum generation length is 100,000 tokens , including thinking. We also experimented with a thinking-budget approach that truncates the reasoning and issues a follow-up call to force a final answer. A response that reaches the limit without a parseable final answer is scored as incorrect, the same as any other wrong answer. 14. Do the evaluated models use extended reasoning ("thinking")? Yes — Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B uses thinking mode, and openai/gpt-oss-120b uses low , medium , and high reasoning efforts. 15. Are there countries where prizes cannot be paid? Prizes are paid by international bank wire transfer. Because of sanctions and banking restrictions, we cannot pay prizes to the following: any person or entity listed on an applicable US, EU or UN sanctions list ; recipients resident in, or with a receiving bank located in, North Korea, Russia, Belarus, Iran, Cuba, Syria, Crimea and other temporarily occupied regions of Ukraine ; recipients resident in, or with a receiving bank located in, a country on the FATF high-risk jurisdictions lists or the EU list of high-risk third countries , as in force at the time of payment. The restriction depends on country of residence and location of the receiving bank, not on citizenship. In practice, transfers to banks in these countries are very likely to be blocked or returned, so we will not attempt them. We recommend that prize winners open a Wise account for receiving prizes (we are not affiliated with Wise, and it does not guarantee that a transfer will succeed). If a winner cannot provide us with a working means of receiving a wire transfer after two unsuccessful transfer attempts , we reserve the right not to award the prize. 16. How do I submit my technical report, and how many submissions are allowed? Technical reports are submitted through the AIMO-Interp Challenge OpenReview forum . All authors must have an OpenReview profile before the report is submitted. We allow only one submission per first author. Each OpenReview submission must list the Codabench submission IDs it describes; we check that these are linked to the same Codabench user ID, and where a first author has several OpenReview submissions we only consider the first one. If a submission is eligible for a prize, the first author will additionally need to complete identity verification matching their name with the OpenReview author. 17. What if I have more questions? Reach out to us on AIMO-Interp Discord or via aimo.interp@gmail.com or open an issue in the getting-started repository . This FAQ will be updated regularly. AIMO Interpretability Challenge · Proposal paper · Getting started · aimo.interp@gmail.com const navLinks = document.querySelectorAll('nav ul li a[href^="#"]');
    const sections = Array.from(navLinks)
        .map(link => document.querySelector(link.getAttribute('href')))
        .filter(Boolean);

    function onScroll() {
        const scrollY = window.scrollY + 80; // offset for fixed nav height
        let current = sections[0];
        for (const section of sections) {
            if (section.offsetTop <= scrollY) {
                current = section;
            }
        }
        navLinks.forEach(link => {
            link.classList.toggle(
                'active',
                link.getAttribute('href') === '#' + current.id
            );
        });
    }

    window.addEventListener('scroll', onScroll, { passive: true });
    onScroll(); // set on page load

    const LEADERBOARD_API = 'https://vps.josefkuchar.com/leaderboards';
    const leaderboardTracks = ['main', 'small'];
    const leaderboardBaselines = { main: 0.52, small: 0.5 };
    const leaderboardViews = Object.fromEntries(leaderboardTracks.map(track => [track, {
        state: document.getElementById(`leaderboard-${track}-state`),
        table: document.getElementById(`leaderboard-${track}-table`),
        body: document.getElementById(`leaderboard-${track}-body`)
    }]));
    const leaderboardMeta = document.getElementById('leaderboard-meta');
    const leaderboardUpdated = document.getElementById('leaderboard-updated');
    let leaderboardData = null;

    const percentFormatter = new Intl.NumberFormat(undefined, {
        style: 'percent',
        maximumFractionDigits: 1
    });

    function formatSubmissionDateTime(value) {
        const date = new Date(value);
        if (Number.isNaN(date.getTime())) return null;
        return {
            date: date.toLocaleDateString(undefined, {
                month: 'short',
                day: 'numeric',
                year: 'numeric'
            }),
            time: date.toLocaleTimeString(undefined, {
                hour: '2-digit',
                minute: '2-digit'
            }),
            exact: date.toLocaleString()
        };
    }

    function safeProfileUrl(value) {
        try {
            const url = new URL(value);
            return ['http:', 'https:'].includes(url.protocol) ? url.href : null;
        } catch {
            return null;
        }
    }

    function createBaselineRow(track) {
        const row = document.createElement('tr');
        row.className = `leaderboard-baseline ${track}`;
        row.setAttribute('aria-label', `${track === 'main' ? 'Main Track' : 'Small Models Track'} baseline: ${percentFormatter.format(leaderboardBaselines[track])}`);

        const rankCell = document.createElement('td');
        rankCell.className = 'rank';
        rankCell.textContent = '—';
        row.appendChild(rankCell);

        const labelCell = document.createElement('td');
        labelCell.className = 'participant';
        labelCell.textContent = 'Baseline';
        row.appendChild(labelCell);

        const referenceCell = document.createElement('td');
        referenceCell.className = 'submitted';
        referenceCell.textContent = 'Reference';
        row.appendChild(referenceCell);

        const scoreCell = document.createElement('td');
        scoreCell.className = 'score';
        scoreCell.textContent = percentFormatter.format(leaderboardBaselines[track]);
        row.appendChild(scoreCell);

        return row;
    }

    function renderLeaderboard(track) {
        if (!leaderboardData) return;

        const view = leaderboardViews[track];
        const entries = leaderboardData.leaderboards[track] || [];
        const fragment = document.createDocumentFragment();
        const baseline = leaderboardBaselines[track];
        let baselineInserted = false;

        entries.forEach((entry, index) => {
            const score = Number(entry.accuracy);
            if (!baselineInserted && (!Number.isFinite(score) || score <= baseline)) {
                fragment.appendChild(createBaselineRow(track));
                baselineInserted = true;
            }

            const row = document.createElement('tr');

            const rankCell = document.createElement('td');
            rankCell.className = 'rank';
            rankCell.textContent = String(index + 1);
            row.appendChild(rankCell);

            const participantCell = document.createElement('td');
            participantCell.className = 'participant';
            const profileUrl = safeProfileUrl(entry.owner_url);
            if (profileUrl) {
                const profileLink = document.createElement('a');
                profileLink.href = profileUrl;
                profileLink.target = '_blank';
                profileLink.rel = 'noopener noreferrer';
                profileLink.textContent = entry.owner_name || 'Unknown participant';
                participantCell.appendChild(profileLink);
            } else {
                participantCell.textContent = entry.owner_name || 'Unknown participant';
            }
            row.appendChild(participantCell);

            const submittedCell = document.createElement('td');
            submittedCell.className = 'submitted';
            const submitted = formatSubmissionDateTime(entry.submission_time);
            if (submitted) {
                const dateLine = document.createElement('span');
                dateLine.textContent = submitted.date;
                const timeLine = document.createElement('span');
                timeLine.className = 'submission-time';
                timeLine.textContent = submitted.time;
                submittedCell.append(dateLine, timeLine);
                submittedCell.title = submitted.exact;
            } else {
                submittedCell.textContent = '—';
            }
            row.appendChild(submittedCell);

            const scoreCell = document.createElement('td');
            scoreCell.className = 'score';
            scoreCell.textContent = Number.isFinite(score) ? percentFormatter.format(score) : '—';
            row.appendChild(scoreCell);

            fragment.appendChild(row);
        });

        if (!baselineInserted) {
            fragment.appendChild(createBaselineRow(track));
        }

        view.body.replaceChildren(fragment);
        view.state.hidden = true;
        view.table.hidden = false;

        if (!entries.length) {
            view.state.replaceChildren();
            const emptyMessage = document.createElement('p');
            emptyMessage.textContent = 'No ranked submissions yet. Be the first!';
            view.state.appendChild(emptyMessage);
            view.state.hidden = false;
            view.table.hidden = true;
        }
    }

    function renderLeaderboards() {
        leaderboardTracks.forEach(renderLeaderboard);
        leaderboardMeta.hidden = false;
    }

    function updateLastUpdated(value) {
        const date = new Date(value);
        const label = Number.isNaN(date.getTime())
            ? 'just now'
            : date.toLocaleString(undefined, {
                dateStyle: 'medium',
                timeStyle: 'short'
            });
        leaderboardUpdated.replaceChildren();
        const liveDot = document.createElement('span');
        liveDot.className = 'live-dot';
        liveDot.setAttribute('aria-hidden', 'true');
        leaderboardUpdated.append(liveDot, `Last updated: ${label}`);
    }

    async function loadLeaderboard() {
        if (!leaderboardData) {
            leaderboardTracks.forEach(track => {
                const state = leaderboardViews[track].state;
                state.hidden = false;
                state.replaceChildren();
                const loadingMessage = document.createElement('p');
                loadingMessage.textContent = 'Loading the latest standings…';
                state.appendChild(loadingMessage);
            });
        }

        const controller = new AbortController();
        const timeout = window.setTimeout(() => controller.abort(), 10000);

        try {
            const response = await fetch(LEADERBOARD_API, {
                cache: 'no-store',
                signal: controller.signal
            });
            if (!response.ok) throw new Error(`Leaderboard request failed (${response.status})`);

            const data = await response.json();
            if (!data.leaderboards || !Array.isArray(data.leaderboards.main) || !Array.isArray(data.leaderboards.small)) {
                throw new Error('Unexpected leaderboard response');
            }

            leaderboardData = data;
            renderLeaderboards();
            updateLastUpdated(data.last_updated_at);
        } catch (error) {
            if (leaderboardData) {
                leaderboardUpdated.textContent = 'Could not refresh — showing the last loaded standings';
            } else {
                leaderboardTracks.forEach(track => {
                    const state = leaderboardViews[track].state;
                    state.replaceChildren();
                    const errorMessage = document.createElement('p');
                    errorMessage.textContent = 'The live standings could not be loaded right now.';
                    const fallbackMessage = document.createElement('p');
                    const fallbackLink = document.createElement('a');
                    fallbackLink.href = 'https://www.codabench.org/competitions/16180/';
                    fallbackLink.target = '_blank';
                    fallbackLink.rel = 'noopener noreferrer';
                    fallbackLink.textContent = 'View the leaderboard on Codabench';
                    fallbackMessage.appendChild(fallbackLink);
                    state.append(errorMessage, fallbackMessage);
                });
            }
            console.error('Unable to load leaderboard:', error);
        } finally {
            window.clearTimeout(timeout);
        }
    }

    loadLeaderboard();
    window.setInterval(() => {
        if (!document.hidden) loadLeaderboard();
    }, 5 * 60 * 1000);
